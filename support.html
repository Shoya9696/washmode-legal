Which Wash Cycle? Support

Which Wash Cycle? helps compare garment care labels with verified washing-machine courses.

This is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the listed washer manufacturers.

Before using a recommendation

Image analysis provides suggestions only. Compare every suggestion with the complete garment label and confirm the symbols yourself. Always check the garment label and your washer manual before washing.

If your washer is not listed

Only models and courses verified from official manufacturer documentation are included. The app does not guess when information cannot be verified.

Camera or photo problems

You can choose an existing photo or enter care symbols manually if the camera is unavailable. Camera access can be changed in iOS or iPadOS Settings.
