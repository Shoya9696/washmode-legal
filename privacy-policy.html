Which Wash Cycle? Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Which Wash Cycle? does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Camera and photos

Camera captures and photos selected by the user are analyzed on the device to suggest garment care symbols. Images are not uploaded to the developer or any third party.

Data stored on the device

Registered washing machines, saved garments, settings, and decision history are stored locally in the app container. Users can delete this data in the app or by deleting the app.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or an external AI service.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, see the support page.
